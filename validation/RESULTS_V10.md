## Verdicts

**H-V10.1 (the combination beats its best half)** — PASS. AUC 0.856 against `static_only` 0.835, **+2.1 points**; the bar was +2. Per seed: +4.1, +1.3, +3.8, +1.3, +0.0.
**H-V10.2 (the statics contribute)** — PASS. Removing the static block costs **+6.6 points** over 5 shared seeds, against +2.0 when the statics could only reach the model through tree splits. The bar was +4.

**H-V10.3 (no regression) — MARGINAL.** Real data is clean: CPSC-2018 0.691 → 0.689
(−0.3) and Sleep-EDF 0.673 → 0.672 (−0.1), both inside noise. On the synthetic grid the
worst cell is −3.9, under the −5 fail bar but above the −2 pass bar, so this neither passes
nor fails. Six cells sit between −2 and −3.9: all four `amp_regression` sizes, which any
linear base already hurt, and three `static_control` sizes.

`static_control` is the interesting one and is not dismissed here. It is the scenario where
the series is pure noise and only the static block carries signal — precisely the case V10
targets — and V10 makes it 2.0 to 3.1 points *worse*. The likely reason is that the base now
fits the statics **linearly**, and `static_control`'s label is a non-linear function of them,
so the trees inherit a confidently wrong starting point instead of a blank one. That is a
real limitation of moving the statics into a linear layer, and it is the first thing a V11
should look at.

**H-V10.4 (earns its default) — YES**, by the frozen rule: H-V10.1 passed and H-V10.3 did
not fail. `dense_include_static` defaults on, inside the still-opt-in ridge base.

## What changed, in one table

| | V9 — base blind to statics | **V10 — base sees statics** |
|---|---|---|
| series alone | 0.807 | 0.790 |
| statics alone | 0.835 | 0.835 |
| **both together** | 0.827 — *worse than either* | **0.856** |
| statics worth | +2.0 | **+6.6** |

The diagnosis held. The statics were being forced through greedy per-node tree splits — the
mechanism `HEADROOM.md` measured as this architecture's ceiling — while the strong linear
layer could not see them. Given a route into the base, the same information is worth three
times as much, and the combination beats both halves for the first time in seven studies.

The win is not confined to the headline metric, which matters: F1 +2.1, accuracy +3.4,
balanced accuracy +2.2 over `static_only`. A result visible only in AUC would deserve
suspicion. The one place `static_only` still leads is recall (0.775 against 0.742) at worse
precision — BMI alone flags many minutes; adding the ECG makes the model choosier about
which.

## What this does not establish

One dataset, +2.1 points, on 35 subjects where a single seed spans 0.785 to 0.906, and one
of the five seeds gave exactly +0.0. This is a real result and a narrow one.

It also cannot be replicated on the datasets already in this project: CPSC and Sleep-EDF
have statics the signal already encodes, which is why they show −0.3 and −0.1 here. A
second dataset with genuinely exogenous statics is the obvious next requirement, and until
one exists the claim stays where the evidence puts it — demonstrated once, on Apnea-ECG.
