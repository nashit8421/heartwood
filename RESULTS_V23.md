## What this means

**Both extras fail on a second independent real suite.**

Sixteen real datasets across V15 and V23, no effect on either, against two synthetic scenarios written to require exactly these operators — and the control above confirms the study would have seen an effect if one were there.

Per `VALIDATION_V23.md` §5 the honest reading is that **both features work only on tasks constructed to need them, and should be deleted** — completing what `VALIDATION_V15.md` §4 instructed and this project declined to do on one suite's evidence. The synthetic wins stay in the record as what they are: proof the operators function, not proof they are worth carrying.
